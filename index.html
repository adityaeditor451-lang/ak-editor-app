FAQ

Common questions

How do I send my footage?
Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer or WhatsApp — whichever is easiest. Any format works; I'll handle conversion.
How long does delivery take?
Reels: 48 hours. Wedding films: 4–7 days. YouTube and podcasts: 2–3 days. Rush delivery (24 hours) is available as an add-on.
What if I don't like the first cut?
Every package includes revisions. You tell me what to change and I re-cut it — no extra charge within the included rounds.
Do you work outside Kota?
Yes — editing is fully remote. Footage comes in online and the final files go back the same way, anywhere in India.
How is payment handled?
50% advance to lock the slot, 50% on delivery. UPI, bank transfer or however is convenient.
